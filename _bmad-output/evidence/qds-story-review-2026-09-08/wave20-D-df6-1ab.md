### The primary-lens re-derivation (per-issue ceiling worst case)

**Enumeration from the pair's own ceilings.** Resource-bearing boxes sanctioned per PR: 2 charged (`ci-red`, `ci-verdict-unavailable`) + 3 uncharged (`transient`, `lead_failure`, `approval-timed-out`, `overflow`, `orphaned`) + `dispatch-refused` (≤1) + `out-of-scope-diff` (≤1) + `scope-requires-privilege` (≤1) = **8**. The *reachable* maximum is smaller: the charged and uncharged caps cannot both saturate (hitting either stops the loop), and `dispatch-refused`/`scope-requires-privilege` are terminal-on-first-occurrence so at most one of them can appear and only as the last box — my derivation reaches 5; the spec's "five-to-six" is the right conservative neighborhood.

**Shipped admission behavior — verified.** `assertWithinBudget` has exactly one production call site, in `src/fleet/dispatch.ts`'s dispatch path, so **every** dispatch reserves against the per-issue ceiling before any box or in-box classification exists: `dispatch-refused`, `out-of-scope-diff` and `scope-requires-privilege` boxes all really do reserve, exactly as the pair claims. `issueBudgetSnapshot` returns ceiling/spent/reserved with the reservation summed as one ceiling per unsettled run; the `extensions.budget` docblock states `remaining = cap − spent − reserved`, matching the pair's "committed = spent + reserved" framing. `capForIssue` resolves `perIssueOverrides`; the fan-out primitive's `issue-ceiling-insufficient` is the n-arms path and is correctly not borrowed.

**Formula — one contradiction found.** Technical Notes and the ANSWERED open-question block both state `parentSpend + 2 × perAttemptCap + (unchargedCeiling + 3) × perDispatchSpendCap` — with `unchargedCeiling = 3` that is 2 charged + 6 reservation-bearing boxes = the 8-box union exactly, and it is the correct, conservative shape. The DoD's issue-ceiling test leg states `(unchargedCeiling + 2) × perAttemptSpendCap` while citing "(Technical Notes)" — different constant, different rate name (finding W20-1). `perDispatchSpendCap` is defined at first use (the uniform `admittedCeiling = min(keyCap, remaining)` reservation; "one rate, not two"); `perAttemptCap` is glossed only as "the per-attempt budget DF6.1b's AC2 charges", and DF6.1b AC2 charges an attempt *count*, not a µ$ figure (finding W20-9).

**Ladder / escalation / registration — checked.** The four-arm ladder is complete against shipped state: `ApprovalState` has exactly the five readings the ladder names (`pausing`, `awaiting-approval`, `approved`, `rejected`, `timed-out`), `pollWindowOpenedAt` rides the single post-push write, `IMMUTABLE_FIELDS` stamps `createdAt` once per record as claimed. The Observability escalation list covers every terminal classification the ladder and AC2 define (12 of 12) — with one pair-internal omission (`fix-scope-missing`, finding DF6.1b-W20-2). Registration exactness holds: `fixLoop` is registered exactly as quoted (`fixContext: "string"`, `fixOutcome: "string"`), `FieldKind` is the four-member set, `strings` element rules exist, the `schema-rejected … must be a string` and "refusing to write key material" messages match verbatim, the store's public surface is exactly `path`/`lockPath`/`create`/`get`/`set`/`close`/`list`/`listWithAnomalies`, and `assertNoSecret`/`assertKind` are unexported — the scratch-store validate-only route is real (`RunRecordStoreOptions.create`).
