## User Story

As the factory host that already observes every resident run,
I want cheap, typed judgment calls made on the host — whether the resident should compact, whether QA findings deserve deep adjudication, whether a dispatch manifest is sane, whether sweep hotspots are worth briefs —
so that frontier spend concentrates on findings and briefs that survive a cheap prior, with every ask recorded before anything is armed.

Recording-only means the directive is **not written**; when armed, the host writes a schema-valid directive and nothing else touches `/run/steering`. *Negative controls, all MUST fail: a compact signal that bypasses the schema — raw NDJSON, an overloaded `proceed`/`retry` note, an env-var side channel; a fourth steering point minted ("there is no fourth", `steering.ts:101`); and a `run_end`-allowed `compact`.*

## Technical Notes

- **Region ownership on shared files:** `src/phase-engine/steering.ts` is DF1.11's — this story **adds only** the union member + two allowlist entries, mints no fourth point, and owes the re-pin ritual (AC4). `src/fleet/observe.ts` is DF3.8's — **adds only** the optional injected L7 callback (default absent = today's behavior, byte-for-byte). `scripts/fleet.ts` **adds only** the host-side callback wiring on the sweep path. `src/phase-engine/roster/schema.ts`, `src/phase-engine/phases/roster-adapter.ts`, and every never-edit symbol are byte-identical **under this story's diff** (J3 owns an adds-only region on `roster/schema.ts`; its edit is not this story's to fence).
- **Guards/limits (named, not implied):** one ask per resident per tick (idempotent, keyed runId+tick); `L7_ASK_STATE_MAX_CHARS` bounded well under the vendor's 32k state cap (64k/request is J1's documented budget warn); ceiling 1M tokens per leg-stage; corpus + provenance writes reuse `scripts/corpus` `appendRecord` and `src/judgment/emit.ts` — no second capture channel.
- **Considered and rejected: writing the compact decision as a `retry` directive with guidance text.** It would dodge the schema extension but abuse the union's semantics (retry = re-enter a phase post-gate-failure), smuggle a control instruction through prose, and be exactly the schema-bypass AC2 forbids. The adds-only member is the honest cost, priced in AC4.
- **Layer placement:** `src/autonomy/` sits above judgment/phase-engine/providers and imports them one-way; `src/judgment/`'s two-import fence (`tests/judgment/fence.test.ts`) is untouched — the pack is a consumer, never a dependency of the judgment core.
