- **BLOCKER — L3's probe-battery verdict (and its evidence-write failures) never reach `settleRun`: a failed battery ships GREEN** — `tests/fleet/e2e/memory-combined.live.test.ts:849-854`
  - Evidence: the `finally` pushes "probe battery FAILED" / "the probe battery FAILED to run" into `problems` (`:826-834`) and hands `problems` to `writeM23EvidenceQuietly` (`:836-847`), but the `settleRun` call feeds only `destroyFailures`/`bankProblems`/`fleetCleanupProblems(...)`/`keyProblems` — `problems` is in neither branch.   - Why broken: on an otherwise-green round, a battery that throws (ProbeConfigError, network) or measures `allRefused:false` (including the INV-M3-2 no-auth 200 danger) leaves `failure === undefined` and empty cleanup arrays → `settleRun(undefined, [])` → the test PASSES. This directly defeats AC5's "a probe that cannot run is a FAILED round, never a skipped assertion" and hides the m23-revoke-verified evidence-write refusal (a secret-scrub refusal would be a `problems` entry too). - **BLOCKER — L2's in-box kg_recall assertion reads worker stderr that the in-box worker chain structurally destroys; a fully-green round can never go green** — `tests/fleet/e2e/memory-combined.live.test.ts:595-616`
  - Evidence: the leg scans harvested artifact files for the literal `[kg-recall] {event:...}` stderr lines the extension emits (`pi-vs-claude-code-vendor/kg-recall/extensions/kg-recall/kg-recall.ts:40`). But the dispatch_task worker turn runs: engine → `runTeamManifest` → `buildTeamSpawn` selects `bun .../team-lead-driver.ts` for `QDS_TEAM_RUNNER=local` (`cli-anything/src/team-events.ts:43-48`), spawned by `cli-anything/src/runner.ts` with `stdio: ["ignore","pipe","ignore"]` — stderr IGNORED by design (`runner.ts:319-326` and again `:1370-1373`). Inside the driver, the worker pi's stderr is piped into an in-memory buffer and on a successful turn (non-empty text) discarded entirely — only a 400-char tail is logged when stdout is EMPTY (`cli-anything/scripts/team-lead-driver.ts:672-680, 725-727`), and that log goes to the driver's stderr, which the runner ignores. The engine-log tee is caller-level and only sees the ENGINE's own fd-2 writes — `dispatch-task.ts:133-143` states this explicitly ("plain process.stderr.write here would bypass pipeline-run's fd-2 tee (the tee is caller-level, not an OS-level dup)").   - Why broken: when the worker genuinely calls kg_recall and succeeds, zero `kg_recall.call` events exist in any harvested artifact → `expect(calls.length).toBeGreaterThan(0)` (`:615`) fails on a working system (false red on every paid round), and `expect(refusals).toEqual([])` (`:616`) is vacuous — it asserts on data that can never be present, so the no-refusal AC is untested. Failure modes don't help either: a refusal's 400-char tail is dropped at the runner's ignored stderr. The assertion is placed where it cannot see the data.

- **MEDIUM — ttl probe is a cross-host clock comparison with only 1.5 s of skew allowance; gateway clock lag flips the probe to a false negative** — `scripts/e2e/memory-negative-probes.ts:303-317` (+ `:78`)

(Currently masked end-to-end by finding 1, which never surfaces the verdict.)
